# SkyRush - Gameplay Guide

Welcome to **SkyRush**, the premier crash game experience. This guide explains how to play and master the mechanics.

## 🎮 The Basics

### What is a Crash Game?

SkyRush is a fast-paced betting game where:

1. You place a bet before the round starts
2. An aircraft takes flight and the multiplier climbs from 1.00x
3. **You decide when to cash out** before the aircraft crashes
4. If you cash out in time, you win your bet × the current multiplier
5. If you don't cash out before the crash, you lose your bet

**That's it!** Simple concept, high tension, thrilling gameplay.

### Example Round

```
Multiplier:  1.00x  1.25x  1.50x  2.00x  3.00x  5.00x  7.50x  CRASH!
Your Bet:    $10
Potential:   $10    $12.50 $15    $20    $30    $50    $75     LOST

If you cashout at 5.00x:
  Win Amount = $10 × 5.00 = $50
  Profit = $50 - $10 = $40
```

## 🕹️ How to Play

### Step 1: Wait for Next Round

Watch the countdown timer. The game shows you:
- When the next round starts
- The countdown to betting period

### Step 2: Place Your Bet

During the **betting period** (3-5 seconds):

1. Enter your bet amount in the **BET PANEL**
2. Or click a quick amount button: $10, $25, $50, $100, $250
3. Click **PLACE BET**
4. Your balance is immediately deducted
5. Prepare to watch the round!

You can place **up to 2 independent bets** using the two bet panels.

### Step 3: Watch the Flight

Once the round starts:

- The aircraft climbs
- The multiplier rises continuously
- The background evolves (as you climb higher)
- The tension builds...

### Step 4: Make the Call

**Now comes the critical moment:**

Watch the multiplier and decide:

- 🚀 **Keep flying?** Aim for bigger multipliers
- 💰 **Cash out now?** Lock in guaranteed profit

**The longer you wait, the bigger the payout... but the risk increases!**

### Step 5: Cashout or Crash

**If you cashout before the crash:**
- ✅ You WIN
- Your payout = Bet × Cashout Multiplier
- Balance increased immediately
- Appears in history

**If you don't cashout in time:**
- ❌ You LOSE
- Your bet is gone
- Try again next round

## 💡 Strategy Tips

### Conservative Play

**Goal:** Steady, reliable profits

- **Target:** 1.50x - 2.00x multipliers
- **Advantage:** High success rate (crash usually happens later)
- **Downside:** Lower per-win profits
- **Example:** $10 bet → $15-20 profit most rounds

### Balanced Play

**Goal:** Mix wins and big wins

- **Target:** 3.00x - 5.00x multipliers
- **Advantage:** Good middle ground of risk/reward
- **Downside:** Miss some winning opportunities
- **Example:** $10 bet → $30-50 profit when it hits

### High Risk Play

**Goal:** Maximum payout, less frequent wins

- **Target:** 10.00x+ multipliers
- **Advantage:** Massive payouts when successful
- **Downside:** Very rare, often crash before reaching
- **Example:** $10 bet → $100+ profit but risky

### Bet Sizing

- **Start small:** $10-25 per round to learn
- **Scale up:** Increase bet as you get comfortable
- **Never overbet:** Only wager what you can afford to lose
- **Two-bet strategy:** Combine conservative + aggressive bets

## 🤖 Advanced Features

### Auto Bet

Let the game automatically place your bet for you each round:

1. Set your bet amount
2. Enable "Auto Bet"
3. Check the checkbox to activate
4. Game places that amount every round

**Tips:**
- Useful for regular play patterns
- Disable before big changes
- Works across multiple rounds

### Auto Cashout

Automatically cash out at a target multiplier:

1. Enter your target (e.g., "2.50")
2. Game cashes out automatically when reached
3. Removes emotion from decisions
4. Guarantees minimum profit at that level

**Tips:**
- Set conservative to guarantee wins
- Set aggressive to aim for big payouts
- Combine with auto-bet for hands-free play

## 🏆 Winning Strategies

### The "Ladder" Method

Increase bet after each win, reset on loss:

1. Start: $10 bet
2. Win at 2.00x? Now bet $20
3. Win at 2.00x? Now bet $40
4. Lose? Back to $10
5. Repeat

**Pros:** Compound wins, manage risk
**Cons:** Requires discipline

### The "Safe + Spicy" Method

Use two bets each round:

- **Bet Panel 1:** Conservative ($10, target 2.00x, auto-cashout)
- **Bet Panel 2:** Aggressive ($20, target 5.00x, manual)

Result: Guaranteed small win + chance at big win

### The "Patience" Method

- Wait for obvious patterns
- Play selectively, not every round
- Larger bets on your best reads
- Skip rounds that feel uncertain

**Requires:** Experience and discipline

## ⚠️ Important Reminders

### House Edge

- SkyRush is designed with slight house advantage
- Over many rounds, house wins edge
- Short-term luck varies wildly
- Bankroll management is crucial

### Crash Point Reality

- Crash points are **random but determined**
- No pattern to predict next crash
- Each round is independent
- Previous rounds don't affect next

### Risk Management

- **Never bet more than you can afford to lose**
- Set loss limits and stick to them
- Take breaks if emotions run high
- Treat winnings as bonus, not income

## Troubleshooting

### Round Won't Start

- Wait for countdown to finish
- Make sure you placed a bet
- Check internet connection
- Refresh page if stuck

### Crash Happened Too Fast

- This happens! Part of the excitement
- Adjust your target multiplier lower
- Use auto-cashout for safer strategy
- Each round is independent

### Balance Didn't Update

- Refresh the page
- Check admin for transaction logs
- Report to support if issue persists

### Can't Place Bet

- Check you have sufficient balance
- Verify bet is within min/max
- Make sure betting period is open
- Try clearing browser cache
